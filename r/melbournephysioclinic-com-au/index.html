Our Services

At Melbourne Physio Clinic, we specialise in treating a wide range of injuries and addressing various forms of pain to help you regain optimal health and mobility. Learn more about the types of consultations and services offered at Melbourne Physio Clinic.

Some of the most common injuries we treat are:

  • Lumbar Facet Joint Pain
  • Lumbar Disc Pain
  • Shoulder Injuries
  • Ankle Injuries
  • Neck and Back Pain
  • Cervical Facet Joint Pain
  • Migraines and Headaches
  • Muscle and Nerve Pain
  • Sports Injuries (sprains, strains, tears)
  • Rehabilitation from surgery or broken bones
  • Work Injuries

We also offer the following services:

Biomechanical Analysis

Utilising in clinic assessment to determine your intrinsic risk factors for individualised biomechanics.

Sports Injury Screening

Analysing the sport specific demands and then combining this with the intrinsic and extrinsic risk factors of your body.

Pre and Postpartum Care

Pre and postpartum pelvic and lumbar pain are preventable and manageable in almost all circumstances.

Bike Set Up Assessment

The biomechanics of a cyclist can vary greatly depending on the cyclist’s experience, discipline specifics and volume of cycling.

Swimming Technique Analysis

Assessment of swimming technique at varying levels of fatigue and must be combined with in-clinic swimming specific assessment.

Gym Technique Assessment

The number of injuries sustained in the gym has forced our hand to offer a gym exercise technique specific assessment and guidance.

Running Technique Analysis

Assessing a runner's technique on the right surface & varying degrees of fatigue combined with an athlete in room screening.

Injury Ergonomics Intervention

Ergonomics are an essential piece of the rehabilitation and injury prevention program. An individualised assessment provides specificity to a generic ergonomic assessment.
