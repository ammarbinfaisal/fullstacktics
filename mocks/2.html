Start where it matters.
Foundation, visibility, then demand.

Growth is a sequence, not a switch.

Ads cannot rescue a confusing store. A great store needs to be found. Search visibility needs a destination worth choosing. We work through the order that lets each layer do its job.

The path

Build a sales channel in the right order.

There is no fixed package. The phases are a way to find the work that deserves attention first—whether you are starting fresh or rebuilding what is already there.

  1. 01

    Make the foundation feel ready.

    Set up or improve the WooCommerce store, then give every key surface the clarity and consistency it needs to earn confidence.

    • WooCommerce setup or rebuild
    • Digital design that supports the sale
    • Clear product and conversion paths
  2. 02

    Make it easier to find.

    Build useful search foundations into the shop and website so the people already looking for what you offer have a better route in.

    • SEO direction and on-site improvements
    • Search-led page structure
    • Content that answers real intent
  3. 03

    Turn up the demand.

    Run PPC campaigns through the channels that fit the audience and buying journey, connecting each click to a page prepared to do its part.

    • Meta Ads and Google Ads
    • Campaign-to-page alignment
    • Practical testing and iteration
