The pattern

Good traffic, wrong page. Good page, no traffic.

Ad spend lands on the homepage

Every campaign points at one generic page that answers none of the searches. You pay full price for the click and lose the visitor in the first three seconds.

A redesign that wiped out rankings

The new site looks better and ranks worse. URLs changed without redirects, content was cut, and the pages Google trusted are now gone.

Nobody can say which channel works

Ads, search, and the site report numbers that don't agree. Without conversion tracking you can't tell what to cut, so budget goes where the loudest report says.

Approach

Measure first, then spend.

  1. Audit

    Audit what you have

    We go through the site, the ad accounts, and the analytics, and you get a written list of what is broken, what is wasting money, and what to do first.

  2. Track

    Fix the tracking

    Conversions are set up and checked before a single budget changes. Until we can see which clicks become leads, every decision is a guess.

  3. Build

    Build and launch

    We ship the pages, campaigns, and technical fixes in that order of payoff. Redirects and rankings are protected through every launch.

  4. Grow

    Report, then hand over

    Monthly reports in plain numbers: traffic, cost, leads. The accounts, code, and analytics stay in your name from day one, so you can leave whenever you like.
